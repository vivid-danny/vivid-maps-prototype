# VividSeats Seating Map — Product Context

## register
product

## Product Purpose
An interactive seating map that lets ticket buyers browse sections, rows, and seats at a live event venue, filter listings by location, and purchase tickets with confidence. The map is the primary decision surface — not a supporting widget.

## Users
Ticket buyers on VividSeats. Desktop and mobile. Context ranges from casual browsing (checking prices for an upcoming game) to final-moment purchase decisions. The user knows what event they want; the map helps them decide where to sit and at what price.

The emotional state: anticipation. They're about to commit money to a memory. The interface should match that gravity — it's not a shopping cart, it's a seat selection ritual.

## Brand Personality
Sharp, premium, decisive.

- **Sharp**: clean geometry, precise alignment, no visual noise. Every pixel earns its place.
- **Premium**: considered spacing, restraint over maximalism. GT Walsheim already signals quality — honor it.
- **Decisive**: clear hierarchy, bold CTAs, unambiguous interactive states. Fast visual feedback. No second-guessing required.

## Tone
Confident without being loud. The map does the talking — UI chrome should recede, not compete with the venue geometry.

## Anti-references
- **StubHub / generic resale gray**: flat, corporate, utility-first — feels like a database, not an experience
- **Ticketmaster dark mode**: heavy, dense, enterprise — wrong emotional register
- **Mobile game UI**: neon, gamified badges, excessive motion — too loud for a purchase decision

## Emotional Goal
The user should feel confident and in control. They understand exactly what they're looking at, where the value is, and what happens when they tap "Checkout." No confusion, no hesitation.
